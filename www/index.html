Swayambhu Monkey Chase

Walk the kora around the great stupa on the hill above Kathmandu. The temple monkeys want your pooja offerings. Keep them off long enough and the karma adds up.

Monkeys watch from the pipal trees along the path and leap down when you pass, so be ready to swing. Climb the 365 steps level by level, or walk the endless kora. If a monkey snatches your thali you get a few seconds to chase it down: hit it, or grab it back when it stops to tease you. Every theft costs you karma (at least 20, or 15% of what you have). Kick a monkey twice to send it running. Kick it only once and it gets angry and attacks you: block it or hit it as it winds up, because if it scratches you you're dazed and the other monkeys rush in for your thali. Collect fruit and toss it to monkeys, so they grab the fruit instead of your thali and run off to climb a tree. Pick up sticks, pebbles, fruit and bells along the path, and earn more by scaring monkeys in a row. Brush along the prayer wheels at the back of the path for a short blessing shield. Hold your guard and keep your offerings safe.
